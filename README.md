# Haowan! 好玩

**Haowan!** is a Chinese-learning (Hanzi) app with gamification, built to close the gap between the high demand for learning Mandarin and how hard it is to learn. Instead of teaching characters as isolated units to memorize, it sets learners inside the world of imperial China as a scholar whose job is to write for the people — giving every character and phrase a purpose within a story, not just a flashcard to clear.

## Background

Mandarin is the second most spoken language in the world, with over 30 million people currently learning it as a foreign language. At the same time, it is consistently ranked among the hardest languages for foreign speakers, due to its tonal system, its lack of a phonetic alphabet, and the sheer volume of characters needed for basic literacy.

## Problem Statement

- People still find it hard to learn Mandarin and don't understand the nuance behind characters — why they're built the way they are, and how components combine to carry meaning — so characters feel arbitrary rather than logical.
- People still rely on rote learning, repeating characters until they stick, which is slow, easy to forget, and disconnected from any real use of the language.

## Product Requirements

- Provide tips during writing that guide without revealing the full answer.
- Use simple and easy-to-understand materials.
- Distinguish between correct and incorrect Hanzi writing.
- Track how long users take to write each character.
- Avoid pressuring users to memorize Hanzi.
- Provide the necessary feedback for users to understand their incorrect writing.
- After grading, show the correct character and a brief reason for mistakes (e.g., missing stroke, wrong radical placement) so users understand what went wrong.
